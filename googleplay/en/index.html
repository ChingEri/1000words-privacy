Learning and local data

The app stores your selected languages, theme, vibration setting, vocabulary range, word levels and statistics, current question and first-attempt result, selected age group, advertising privacy choice, and temporary banner-free expiry on your device. This supports continued learning and saved preferences.

The developer operates no app registration, user account, backend, or cloud-sync service. Our code does not send learning answers or progress to advertising SDKs as learning events. This does not mean that third-party app components process no data.

System pronunciation

When you tap the speaker, the original word and selected language are passed to the installed Android TextToSpeech provider. The app selects an installed voice of the same language that the engine marks as offline. It does not silently switch to a network voice. If a suitable voice is unavailable, it offers system settings.

The microphone is not used, and the app does not record your speech or save audio recordings. The TTS provider is a separate component with its own practices. An offline voice flag does not prove that the engine has no telemetry or other network activity. Android controls engine selection, voice installation, and media volume.

Advertising and your choices

The age group is stored on the device; no date of birth or identity documents are requested. In version 1.3.3, age determines ad request restrictions. Unknown age uses conservative processing without personalization and is not treated as verified adulthood. The app is intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and adults. A protective mode for under-13 users does not change its target audience.

Before Google Mobile Ads initialization, the app applies age restrictions and a maximum ad content rating of G. Under 13, ages 13–15 and unknown age use the stricter Child treatment; ages 16–17 use Teen. These settings restrict advertising data processing and do not mean all teenagers are classified as children under 13. Personalization is disabled for these profiles. Ages 18+ use Unspecified, with personalization subject to applicable consent and the user setting. Users can additionally disable personalization in app settings.

Google UMP updates consent with a separate under-age-of-consent signal. Requests are allowed only when the actual CanRequestAds result is true; non-personalized advertising does not replace required consent. After initialization completes and before loading ads, publisher first-party ID is disabled for every profile. Available forms and advertising privacy options depend on UMP, region and ad-console configuration. The age choice is not identity verification or legally verified age.

Changing age or ad privacy destroys previously loaded ads before applying the new settings. When requests are permitted and an ad is available, the banner is shown from the home screen and during the marathon. Only a confirmed reward from voluntary rewarded viewing disables the banner for 3600 seconds; the expiry is saved and no banner is requested during that hour. Learning remains available when no ad is available.

This edition uses Google Mobile Ads Unity 11.5.0, Android 25.4.0 and Google UMP 4.0.0. The advertising SDK may process IP addresses and approximate region, ad interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Restricting personalization does not mean technical processing stops. Google's current disclosure describes 25.5.0; practices of the installed 25.4.0 need to be checked with the provider. Read more: Google Privacy Policy and SDK data disclosure.

Storage, backups, and deletion

Local state remains until app data or the local copy is removed. Android Auto Backup is not disabled: the operating system may back up and restore app files depending on the device, account, and backup settings. The developer operates no separate backup service.

You can clear local progress through Android’s app settings → storage → clear data. This resets learning and preferences. Manage operating-system backups separately in Android. Uninstalling the app does not guarantee removal of backups or prevent later restoration.

Deleting local files does not automatically delete information held by advertising, email, or system providers. Their retention and deletion procedures follow the relevant provider’s terms and applicable settings.

Support and data requests

Contact developer opens your email app. You send the message yourself; the app does not automatically attach statistics or logs. The developer receives the sender address, content, and attachments you choose to send in order to respond.

Correspondence is used to resolve your request and related follow-up questions. It is kept while the issue is being resolved and for no more than 90 days after closure, then removed from email folders and saved attachments under the developer’s control. Longer retention is permitted only for a specific legal necessity, limited to the necessary information and period. You may request deletion in a free-form email to this edition’s contact, preferably from the address used in the correspondence. If you use another address, proportionate verification of ownership of the correspondence is used; a passport is not requested by default. Requests are handled without undue delay and answered within one month of receipt, or sooner where applicable law requires. If some information must be retained, the developer explains the reason and scope. The developer does not control the email provider’s internal backups and does not promise immediate deletion from them; their retention and deletion follow the provider’s rules.

For access, correction, or deletion requests concerning information received by the developer, write to intermantunarili@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or confidential information that is unnecessary to resolve your request.

Permissions and external services

The app uses network access, network availability checks, short vibration, and advertising SDK capabilities, including advertising-ID permission. Identifier availability depends on Android and your settings. Camera, microphone, contacts, and precise-location permissions are not requested. Vibration can be disabled in the app.

Opening this policy, an email client, or system settings takes you to another service. Advertising, TTS, email, browser, and backup providers may have their own network activity. The developer does not promise that all these components operate without network exchange.

Audience and distribution

The app is intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and adults: Google Play target groups are 13–15, 16–17, and 18 or older. The first release is not directed at children under 13. The content rating is determined separately through the store’s questionnaire.

Distribution is planned in countries and regions available to the developer’s account where the app and its advertising integration meet applicable requirements. Actual availability is determined by Google Play settings after regional review and may be limited. This policy does not certify compliance in every country.

Policy changes

This page is updated with a new date when app functionality, providers, or data practices change. The policy for your edition is available from app settings. Questions about this policy can be sent to intermantunarili@gmail.com.
